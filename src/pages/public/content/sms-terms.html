How Zylove uses text messages, and how to stop them.

01 The program

Zylove notifications is the text message program of Zylove, a dating app for adults 18+ operated by Zylove, LLC.

02 What we text

  • One-time verification codes — sent only when you sign in with your phone number.
  • Account and security alerts — for example a failed payment, a moderation decision about your photos, or a notice about your account.
  • Match and message notifications — a new match, a new message or a new like, only if you opt in.
  • Staff alerts — Zylove staff who opt in receive operational alerts.

Notification texts are optional and never a condition of using Zylove. You opt in by ticking the text notifications box in Settings → Notifications (or when we ask during onboarding) — see below. We don't send marketing or promotional texts.

03 How to opt in

Signed in, open Settings → Notifications and switch text notifications on. This box appears, unticked; texts start only after you tick it and tap Turn on:

We record when you opted in, the number and the wording you agreed to, and text you once to confirm.

04 Frequency and cost

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

05 Stopping texts and getting help

Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time. Reply HELP for help. After STOP you'll get one message confirming you're unsubscribed, and no further notification texts. You can also turn texts off anytime in Settings → Notifications. To start again, reply START or turn texts back on in Settings.

Support: support@zylove.app

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

06 Privacy

Mobile phone numbers and SMS opt-in consent are never shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

See our Privacy Policy for how we handle your phone number, and our Terms of Service (section 3.4) for the terms that apply to these messages.
