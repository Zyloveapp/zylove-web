On-device message checks Your device checks the messages you receive for common scam patterns — requests for money, gift cards or a verification code, or pressure to move to another app — and shows you a safety note. This happens only on your device; nothing about your messages is sent to us. During an account's first 48 hours it can't send links, and a link it sent shows the recipient a safety note; for that, your public profile records when your first 48 hours end (to the hour). Founding members are exempt. Your device also stops phone numbers, handles and email addresses being sent in chat, and hides any that arrive — swapping details goes through Share contact instead.
Share contact Once you've both sent at least 3 messages, either of you can offer a contact card with the details you choose (phone number, Instagram, Snapchat or WhatsApp). The card is end-to-end encrypted on your device — Zylove can't read it — and it is only sent if the other person accepts. Either of you can take cards back, which removes them from the chat for both of you; the other person may already have saved them. We record when contact requests are made and how often (never the details) as a safety signal.
Duplicate-photo checks When you upload a profile photo, our servers make a short fingerprint of it (a "perceptual hash" — a number, not an image) to spot the same or a near-identical photo on another account, and photos from accounts banned for scams. A match on another account sends both accounts to a person for review; it never removes a photo by itself. A match with a banned scam account's photo holds the photo back until a person reviews it. For an account banned for scams or fraud, we keep its photo fingerprints together with the account's ID and display name, whether it was banned for a scam, and how many reports of each kind it received, so our team can recognize the photos if they're uploaded again and see why. We keep these for as long as the ban stands and delete them if the ban is overturned.
Reports and evidence When you report someone you can attach messages from that chat, and photos if you choose. Only the messages you select leave your device; they reach our safety team unencrypted so a person can review your report. We keep them encrypted in a restricted evidence locker that only our safety team can open, and every time it is opened is logged. The person you report isn't told about the report at all. You can download your own copy of what you sent as a PDF — we make it when you ask and don't keep it.
Message verification (franking) Each message you send carries a cryptographic fingerprint so that, if a message is ever reported, we can confirm it is exactly what was sent and who sent it. We can't read your messages from these fingerprints. The trade-off: if someone reports a message you sent and attaches it, you can't deny to us that you sent it — that is what makes reports trustworthy. Messages sent before this feature are treated as unverified.
Messages to Zylove curated profiles During a city's launch, some profiles are Zylove curated profiles (see our Terms, section 2.4). Messages you send them are not end-to-end encrypted, and they are checked for the same scam patterns. The most recent ones (up to 8) are sent to Anthropic to write the curated profile's reply, and your profile name and bio are used to write its first message. If one matches, we keep only the matching excerpt (at most 200 characters) for 90 days, for a person to review.
Photo checks Your profile photos are checked for signs of being AI-generated or deepfaked (Sightengine), and searched for on public web pages to find stolen photos (Google Cloud Vision). A match only sends the account to a person for review — it never removes a photo or an account by itself. The results are kept with your account and deleted with it.

03 How we use your data

To operate the app

  • Create and maintain your profile
  • Show your profile to compatible users in discovery feeds
  • Calculate compatibility scores between users
  • Enable matched users to communicate via encrypted chat
  • Deliver your curated daily queue of 5 profiles

To keep Zylove safe

  • Detect and remove fake profiles, spam, and abusive behavior
  • Process reports and take action against violating accounts
  • Run our behavioral reputation system — users who treat people well are rewarded
  • Review accounts flagged by our safety checks. A person reviews every flag, and any action is graduated — for example showing a profile less often in Explore while it's reviewed, or a suspension
  • Recognize accounts used from the same device, or from a device used by a banned account
  • Suspend an account until a person reviews it when two people report it as a scam within 30 days — only if those two accounts are each at least 48 hours old and not linked to each other by device or network. This is never a ban
  • In cities where we turn it on, limit likes and chat photos during a new account's first week
  • Let you appeal a suspension once: a person reviews your appeal and the decision is recorded
  • Make sure everyone is 18 or older: your age is checked when you sign up and again by our servers

To power AI features

  • Generate your bio using profile data sent to the Anthropic Claude API
  • Provide AI-powered profile coaching and compatibility insights
  • Generate personalized onboarding questions based on your profile
AI data use: Profile data sent to Anthropic for bio generation and coaching is processed per Anthropic's API data policy. We do not send your phone number to Anthropic, and we never send messages between members. Messages you send to a Zylove curated profile are the exception: the most recent ones (up to 8) are sent to Anthropic to write the curated profile's reply, and your profile name and bio are used to write its first message. Your religion and political views are never used in the bio we write for you. Profile text data (bio, prompts, preferences) is always transmitted for bio generation and coaching. Profile photos are transmitted to Anthropic only when you explicitly opt in to photo analysis in Settings → Privacy. You can withdraw this consent at any time.

To process payments

Web subscriptions (Spark+ and Elite) are processed by Stripe. Your card details are entered on Stripe's secure checkout and go directly to Stripe — Zylove never receives or stores your card number. We receive your subscription status and a Stripe customer reference so we can apply your plan.

To communicate with you

  • Send text messages (SMS) — only the kinds described under Text messages below
  • Respond to support and safety inquiries

05 Data sharing

With other users

Your public profile — name, age, photos, bio, intent, and any lifestyle data you choose to show — is visible to other Zylove users in discovery and on your profile. You control visibility toggles for most fields.

Your profile also shows the month you joined ("Member since") and, once at least 5 people have messaged you first, "Usually replies" if you answer most of them. We never show a reply percentage.

Your phone number, exact location, email address, who you're attracted to, and your religion and political views are never shown to other users unless you voluntarily include them in your bio. They are used only for matching. If you choose "Don't show on my profile" for your gender identity, it isn't shown either. Someone who likes you without a match stays anonymous: you see their photo and what they wrote, but not their name, age, location, gender, relationship status or kids until you link.

Blocking applies to your whole account. When you block someone, in Spark or in Play, neither of you can see or contact the other in either mode.

With law enforcement

We may disclose your information to law enforcement or regulatory authorities if required by law, court order, or to prevent imminent harm. Tier 3 reports (involving minors, non-consensual content, or criminal activity) may trigger mandatory reporting obligations.

Business transfers

If Zylove is acquired, merges with another company, or transfers substantially all of its assets, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you via email or in-app notice before your data is subject to a different privacy policy.

06 Security

Message encryption

Messages between people using the Zylove web app are end-to-end encrypted using NaCl (TweetNaCl). They are encrypted on your device before transmission and can only be decrypted by the intended recipient — Zylove cannot read them (see the chat PIN backup below for the one exception). If you report someone and the chat later ends, that chat stays readable — only to you, and still end-to-end encrypted — for 30 days so you can use it in your report; then it is deleted.

When someone in a conversation hasn't set up encryption (for example, some mobile accounts), messages in that conversation are stored protected by access controls — only the people in the conversation can access them — rather than end-to-end encrypted.

If you set a chat PIN, an encrypted copy of your chat key is stored with us so you can read your messages on a new device. It is encrypted on your device with a key derived from your PIN; we never receive your PIN or your unencrypted key, and we only release the encrypted copy after a correct PIN check, with lockouts after repeated wrong attempts. Because a 4-digit PIN has only 10,000 combinations, this backup protects against someone who gets into your account, but is not as strong as end-to-end encryption on its own: someone with access to our database could in principle recover your key from it, and with it your messages. You can choose not to set a chat PIN.

If you report a conversation or choose to share content with us, we may review what you share for safety purposes.

Screenshot protection

Zylove cannot block screenshots on web browsers. We encourage users to only share what they are comfortable with.

Play PIN

Switching to Play requires a 4-digit Play PIN privacy lock, with automatic session lock after 2 minutes of inactivity. The PIN is checked by our servers, which keep only a salted hash of it and limit wrong attempts. It hides Play from other people using your device; it is not a substitute for keeping your account secure — whoever controls your phone number can reset it by text.

Infrastructure security

All data is stored in Google Firebase (SOC 2 Type II certified). Data in transit is encrypted via TLS 1.2+. Data at rest is encrypted by Google Cloud infrastructure.

Security issue? Please report vulnerabilities responsibly to support@zylove.app. We take all security reports seriously and respond within 48 hours.

07 Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account:

  • Your profile is removed from discovery immediately
  • Your photos are deleted from Firebase Storage within 12 months
  • Your chats end immediately. The people you were talking to keep a read-only copy of the conversation, shown as "Deleted User", until it's permanently deleted within 12 months
  • A recovery record (your phone number, name, birthday, gender identity, pronouns, bio, photo links and moderation status), so you can restore your account within 90 days and so a locked age or gender can't be changed by signing up again. It's deleted 18 months after deletion; for a banned account it's kept for as long as the ban stands
  • Safety and moderation records — reports, reviews, unmatch reasons, Zylove Score events, safety review queue entries, vibe checks and blocks: up to 2 years, to prevent ban evasion
  • Terms acceptance records (date, document versions, IP address and browser user agent): while your account exists and for 12 months after you delete it
  • Hashed device and network data: 90 days (for a banned account, as long as the ban stands)
  • Safety scores are recalculated daily and deleted with your account; closed safety reviews are kept for 2 years
  • First-message fingerprints: 14 days
  • A reported chat kept for the reporter: 30 days after the chat ends
  • Records of what our team views and does on accounts: 2 years. Every view, download and action by our team is logged
  • Signup country (country codes only): as long as your account exists
  • Excerpts of scam-like messages sent to Zylove curated profiles: 90 days
  • Photo check results: deleted with your account
  • Report evidence: 30 days after our decision, or after the outcome of an appeal. Child-safety cases are kept for 1 year, and anything under a legal hold or a law-enforcement preservation request is kept until the hold ends. Evidence we never decide on is deleted after 180 days
  • Decision records (the decision, category, date and account IDs — no message content), and appeals: 2 years
  • Message fingerprints (no content): while the chat exists, and 30 days after it is deleted
  • Photo fingerprints (perceptual hashes — not images): while the photo exists. For an account banned for scams or fraud, its fingerprints, ID, display name, whether it was banned for a scam and its report counts are kept as long as the ban stands, so the same photos can be recognized if they're uploaded again, and deleted if the ban is overturned
  • Ended connections (names, dates and message counts — never messages), so you can still report or review someone: 90 days after the match ends
  • Your opt-out from texts (your phone number and when you replied STOP): for as long as we need it to honor your opt-out
  • Free-trial history (a one-way hash of your phone number and your trial dates), so each number gets one free trial: kept for as long as we offer free trials, including after you delete your account
  • Feature-use counters (how many times you used limited features in each period), daily notification counters, and a log that an account was deleted (account ID, date and reason): 2 years
  • What you send us through the website's contact, waitlist and founding-member forms (name, email and message): 12 months
  • Anonymized, aggregated data may be retained indefinitely for product improvement

Certain data may be retained longer if required by law or to resolve active disputes.

09 Children

Zylove is strictly for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. Your age is checked when you sign up and again by our servers.

Reporting suspected minor content triggers an immediate Tier 3 response — the account is suspended pending review and may be referred to law enforcement.
